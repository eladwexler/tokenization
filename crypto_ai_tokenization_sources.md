# Free public sources for crypto, AI, tokenization and investment research

Checked: 2026-10-08.

## Scope and access standard

This is a curated directory of **29 editorial/research crawl sources**, plus dedicated asset-trend references, not an exhaustive list of every website. Tokenization here means blockchain-based asset tokenization, including real-world assets (RWA) and digital securities.

All listed starting pages returned HTTP 200 and readable text through Firecrawl during this session without a publisher login or subscription. These are **free public content sources**; the verification does not imply that Firecrawl itself is free, or that every linked article or product is free. Some publishers also sell subscriptions or data services: crawl only the public sections identified here.

**Crawl permission has not been audited.** Successful retrieval establishes technical accessibility, not permission for an arbitrary crawler. Check each host's current robots.txt and terms before collection. The tests covered the listed entry pages, not every article, pagination path, PDF, or plain-HTTP crawler configuration. Browser rendering may be needed on some sites. No source requiring paid access is included as a crawl target.

## Crawl setup

- Discover article URLs from the listed indexes, permitted sitemaps, or officially published RSS feeds. Feed endpoints were not tested in this session.
- Read robots.txt for the actual hostname and crawler user agent, follow permitted paths, and respect terms and rate limits. Skip prohibited paths, login pages, subscription prompts, CAPTCHAs and paywalls.
- Fetch a sample full article from each source before enabling a scheduled crawl. An accessible index does not prove that its linked articles are accessible.
- Restrict mixed-access publishers to freely readable articles. Skip downloads requiring forms, email addresses or customer access.
- Deduplicate by canonical URL and retain source, title, author, publication/update dates, retrieval time, article URL, topic tags and content type.
- Label project/vendor announcements, investor viewpoints, sponsored stories and independent reporting separately. Preserve disclosures when available.
- Refresh news indexes more often than research archives, using conditional requests where supported. Choose intervals according to publisher rules rather than a fixed aggressive schedule.

## Topic filters

Useful terms: artificial intelligence, AI agents, agentic payments, AI tokens, decentralized AI, DeAI, DePIN, GPU compute, inference, Bittensor, machine-to-machine payments, stablecoins, tokenization, tokenized securities, tokenized equities, tokenized treasuries, real-world assets, RWA, private credit, institutional adoption, venture funding, token economics, unlocks, liquidity and market outlook.

AI-only sources provide context about models, agents and compute demand; they are not dedicated crypto investment publications. RWA coverage concerns asset tokenization and should not be conflated with AI language-model tokenization.

## Exclusions and verification limits

Paid research portals, subscriber-only reports, premium market-data APIs, private communities and account-required dashboards are outside this list. Free editorial sections of mixed-access publishers remain eligible. This directory does not recommend crawling social platforms, scraping authenticated accounts, or bypassing access controls.

Firecrawl temporarily rate-limited verification calls; successful retries supplied the final entries. Failed or unverified targets were omitted. The old Coin Metrics insights URL redirected to a Talos page during testing and was omitted; Variant's tested articles URL returned 404 and was also omitted.

The linked source pages in the tables are the research references and tested crawl seeds. Recheck access and crawl rules before use because publisher policies and page routes can change.

Checked on 2026-10-08: all five CoinPaprika ticker endpoints returned HTTP 200 with the expected asset identity, and all five CoinGecko coin pages returned HTTP 200 with readable content through Firecrawl. Direct access from another network can differ. CoinPaprika robots.txt returned 404 during verification.

- **SOL — Solana:** [CoinPaprika ticker](https://api.coinpaprika.com/v1/tickers/sol-solana); [CoinGecko cross-check](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/solana).
- **LINK — Chainlink:** [CoinPaprika ticker](https://api.coinpaprika.com/v1/tickers/link-chainlink); [CoinGecko cross-check](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/chainlink).
- **ETH — Ethereum:** [CoinPaprika ticker](https://api.coinpaprika.com/v1/tickers/eth-ethereum); [CoinGecko cross-check](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/ethereum).
- **ZEC — Zcash:** [CoinPaprika ticker](https://api.coinpaprika.com/v1/tickers/zec-zcash); [CoinGecko cross-check](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/zcash).

CoinPaprika provides structured USD prices, market cap, volume and provider-reported changes. Its public API does not require an API key. Its [free plan](https://coinpaprika.com/api/pricing/) is for personal/noncommercial projects; other uses have separate terms. The crawler fetches these API endpoints separately from editorial sources. CoinGecko links are manual cross-check references, not additional news crawl seeds.

For objective trend evidence, prefer timestamped numerical data over price predictions. Neither aggregator is a guarantee of accuracy: cross-check prices and definitions, retain provider timestamps and flag stale values. The BTC response was already stale during verification. Several long-horizon percentage-change fields returned zero, so those values should not be interpreted as proof of flat performance.

For news interpretation, prefer established editorial reporting and corroborate substantive claims across independent publications. Project blogs, exchange research, investor theses and sponsored content can be useful primary evidence but carry financial interests. No source in this list is certified neutral or universally trustworthy. The crawler retains provenance and flags detectable sponsored/access-restricted content.
